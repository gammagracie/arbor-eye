Animations

A · Tree settles into the ampersand The engraved tree fades in large above the wordmark, the letters come up, then the tree shrinks and rises into place and turns into the ampersand, staying behind it. 5.3 seconds. The animated header logo of the second homepage, "H2" (Home-animated.dc.html, September 2026; removed 2 October)
B · Tree settles, then fades away As A, but as the tree turns into the ampersand it fades away, leaving the plain wordmark, which eases up 8px to where the static logo sits on every other page. 5.3 seconds. The animated header logo of "H4" (Home-animated-3.dc.html, September 2026; removed 2 October)
C · Engraved tree into the simplified tree The engraved tree fades in at its resting size with the letters, holds, and crossfades into the simplified v3 Tree of Life (outlined 7-leaf crown, solid stem and ampersand), which the logo rests on. Nothing moves. 6.6 seconds. The animated header logo of the homepage until 11 October, kept as index-b.html
D · Homepage intro over the first painting The white Tree of Life fades in over the painting (3.2s), holds, and fades away slowly; part-way through, ARBOR & EYE comes up on a light dark panel, holds, and fades off. About 11 seconds. On the homepage now (index.html) and on H1
