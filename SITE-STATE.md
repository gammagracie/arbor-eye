## Global layout

- **Hover growth** (since 2026-10-21, every page): an image that enlarges on hover (G5's works, G6's column, G7's thumbnails, the Index thumbnails, the grid gallery) grows and shrinks back slowly and softly, 0.9s on a gentle ease both ways (it was 0.3-0.45s and quick to settle). The cursor-following thumbnail swells (the homepage trays and G5's filmstrip) follow the cursor over 0.45s (was 0.14-0.16s) so they stay with it, and the homepage trays settle back over 0.9s when the cursor leaves. Hover titles fade in and out everywhere.
- **Alt text on the image's frame** (every page since 2026-10-20; the homepage versions have had their own version since 2026-10-12): `alt-text.js`, loaded on G5, G6, G7, the Index, the grid gallery (grid, detail view, buy page, lightbox and zoom), About and Contact. Artwork images carry their title and tagline (`data-alt-title`, `data-alt-tag`; the galleries' works lists now carry each tagline as `g`, from `artworks.csv`), so their alt text reads "Title: tagline"; About and Contact photographs use their own descriptive alt text. The browser's own alt text is hidden; instead the words are set centred on the image's frame (title in EB Garamond italic capitals, a colon, the tagline or description in 15px grey; the Index's small thumbnails show the title alone in small type), shown from the start until the image has loaded (since 2026-10-21; they used to wait 0.8s) and kept if it fails to load (a failed image is hidden; on the grid gallery its placeholder stays behind the words); never over another picture already showing in the same place (a crossfade). The words are centred on the image itself, also where the image sits in a box with other content (About's photos beside their text). The homepage versions' own captions likewise show straight away. The pages load `alt-text.js?v=4`: bump the number whenever the script changes so browsers fetch the new one. Screen readers read the real alt text only. Port to Shopify as a small theme snippet with the same rule.
- **Type over 2000px** (since 2026-10-19): on screens wider than 2000px every font size on every page is 25% larger (each `font-size` is multiplied by a `--fs` variable, 1, or 1.25 over 2000px), except the logo's hidden text (the logo is an image) and H0's hover title (sized to its thumbnail). H2 keeps 25% more room for its hover title there. The nav stays on one line beside the logo from 2001px.
- **Header and footer are identical on every page**: same height, same side distance (the standard side padding below), including the home page, where the painting may be wider or narrower than the header.
- **Side padding** (header, content, footer share it): 24px phone, 48px tablet (768–1023px), 10% of the viewport from 1024px up. A stable scrollbar gutter keeps pages aligned whether or not they scroll.
- **Header**: 72px tall, growing to 80px between 1280px and 1920px wide. Logo, nav links (13px, tracked uppercase) and account/bag icons share one baseline on desktop. Active nav item: the page's own link, in the desktop nav and the mobile menu, is bold (700) and black (#141414), no underline, set inline on the link on every page (since 2026-10-19; the old `.on` class did not show on several pages).
