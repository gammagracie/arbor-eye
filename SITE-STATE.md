## Global layout

- **Header and footer are identical on every page**: same height, same side distance (the standard side padding below), including the home page, where the painting may be wider or narrower than the header.
- **Side padding** (header, content, footer share it): 24px phone, 48px tablet (768–1023px), 10% of the viewport from 1024px up. A stable scrollbar gutter keeps pages aligned whether or not they scroll.
- **Header**: 72px tall, growing to 80px between 1280px and 1920px wide. Logo, nav links (13px, tracked uppercase) and account/bag icons share one baseline on desktop. Active nav item is bold, no underline. Phone header is vertically centred. Account and bag icons are thin line icons (a person outline and a square shopping bag) from `assets/icons/account.svg` and `cart.svg`, drawn at 19px in the header (80% of their 24px design size, line weight kept visually the same) and at 24px on the mobile menu rows; `assets/icons/mail.svg` (solid grey envelope) is stored for later use and not placed anywhere yet.
- **Footer**: 43px tall, growing to 54px between 1280px and 1920px (15% shorter than the earlier 50–64px); on phones held in portrait it is twice as tall (86px), content still centred. One line: copyright left, Instagram (links to instagram.com/arbor.eye, new tab) and Pinterest icons right; on very narrow phones the copyright wraps to two lines with "Arbor & Eye" kept together on the second, and stays vertically centred on the icons. Sits at the end of the page (not sticky).
- **Mobile menu** (under 768px): hamburger becomes an X; the menu slides down as a fixed overlay between header and footer, footer pinned to the bottom of the screen, page scroll locked. Items: Gallery, About, Contact, Shipping, Returns & Exchanges, then Login/Account and Shopping Cart rows with icons (account/bag icons leave the header whenever the hamburger shows). Double spacing between items.
- **Fonts**: Neuzeit Grotesk (self-hosted in `assets/fonts/`, loaded through `assets/fonts/neuzeit.css`; Light 300, Regular 400, Bold 700, with 600 resolving to Bold) for all sans text, falling back to Helvetica Neue; EB Garamond (Google Fonts) for headings and work titles.
- **Logo**: the supplied SVG wordmark (`uploads/arbor-eye-wordmark.svg`, from `ART/Logo/Wordmark/SVG`, cropped to the mark) on every page except H2, drawn in the text colour with hover dimming. It is sized so its capitals match the former text logo's cap height (20px), making it 188px wide, and sits on the same baseline; the former text logo is kept in the link invisibly so the header layout is unchanged at every screen size (the link is clipped to the mark's 188px width so the narrowest phones keep logo and hamburger on one row). H2 shows the same wordmark as the final frame of its animation. Licence for Neuzeit Grotesk to be confirmed before launch.
- **Temporary nav items**: the header nav and mobile menu read H1 · Gallery · G4 · G5 · G6 · About · Contact while the alternates are under review (H1 plain-logo home, G4 horizontal scroll, G5 multi-up filmstrip, G6 editorial column; the logo opens the animated-logo home, `index.html`). G3 left the nav on 2026-10-03 (page kept). H2 and H4 were deleted on 2026-10-02 (recoverable from the `pinned-2026-10-02` tag). While they are there, temporary rules tighten the nav below 1280px (12px text; 11px and closer spacing under 900px) so the header stays on one line. Remove the extra items and those rules together.
- **Page titles**: "Arbor & Eye | Artwork by Ned Church" on the home pages, "Arbor & Eye | Gallery" on every gallery version, "Arbor & Eye | About", "Arbor & Eye | Contact". ("Arbor & Eye | Cart" and "Arbor & Eye | Account" are reserved for those pages when they exist; the header's account and bag icons currently link nowhere.)
- **Favicon**: the Tree of Life in the site's ink colour on a transparent square, `assets/favicon.png` (64px) with `favicon.ico` and a 180px `apple-touch-icon.png` (plus a 512px `icon-512.png`), linked from every page.
- **Typography helpers**: `text-wrap: pretty` on all text to avoid single-word last lines.
- **Breakpoints**: phone < 768, tablet 768–1279, desktop ≥ 1280 (gallery grid also switches at 1000, side padding at 1024).

## Gallery, multi-up filmstrip (`gallery-filmstrip-2.html`, "G5")

- A copy of the filmstrip gallery (thumbnails smaller here: 30px tall, 25px on phones, magnifying to about 150% under the cursor rather than 190%; the row glides at up to 220px per second rather than 380, and steers only while the cursor moves: once the cursor rests on a thumbnail the row brakes to a stop within about two seconds so it can be clicked; the chevrons scale with the viewport from 39 x 108px at 1440px down to 20 x 56px, and on phones sit on the page's side padding, level with the logo and the menu button) with a **view switch** at the top left, lined up with the logo (where the grid gallery's switcher sits) (three icons: one solid square, three vertical bars, and a 4 x 3 grid of small squares; the active one black, the rest pale; hidden on phones, which stay one-up). It sets how many works are featured: 1 (exactly the filmstrip page's behaviour), 3 in a row (the default on load), or 12 in three rows of four.
- The featured works form a centred row (or a three-row grid), all the same height, 28px apart; the chevrons hug the set's ends. One-up is 95% of the largest the stage allows; at 3-up the row spans the page's content width exactly, and 12-up is 95% of that size (both for a little more room above and below), so the first work's left edge lines up with the logo and the last work's right edge with the bag icon (unless the works are so tall that the height limits them first). In the grid the rows are 64px apart so a hovered work's title fits beneath it without touching the row below; every landscape is drawn in the same 1.492:1 box so the columns line up (the few wider works, such as Brilliance and Canopy, are trimmed a little at the sides there only), and portraits keep their own narrower shape, centred in the column. When the window moves (arrows, arrow keys, swipes or a thumbnail click) the row slides: the works travel in the direction of the arrow (right arrow: the current works slide off to the right and the next set arrives from the left; left arrow the reverse; a thumbnail click goes the other way round: a work to the right of the featured set slides the row left, arriving from the right, and a work to the left slides it right), together like a belt (0.95s, easing in and out so it starts and stops gently; the incoming images are loaded before anything moves); changing the view crossfades the slots in place instead. Hovering any featured work enlarges it gently (103%) and shows its title directly beneath that work, 25px below it when several works are shown (midway to the thumbnails on one-up; the title slides across as the cursor moves between works), and clicking opens its detail view.
- The featured set is a window over the list: the filmstrip thumbnails of every featured work show at full strength, as does the one under the cursor; the rest sit at 50% (no outline on this page). Clicking a thumbnail slides the window to it (that work becomes the leftmost, unless it is within the last few, when the window stops at the end of the list). Changing the count keeps the first featured work and extends or trims the window from there. Arrows, arrow keys, and dragging anywhere on the stage (mouse or finger, including the white space around the works: the row follows the pointer, a pull of more than 40px slides on in that direction, a shorter pull springs back) move the window by a whole set (three works at 3-up, twelve at 12-up); one-up wraps round at the ends, wider windows stop at them. The URL hash names the first featured work.

## Gallery, editorial column (`gallery-editorial.html`, "G6")

- Standalone plain HTML/CSS/JS page with the same header, footer, side padding and mobile menu; the works list is embedded (same list as the filmstrip) and needs regenerating when the gallery's list changes.
- **Order**: Brilliance opens and Canopy closes (as in the grid); between them the 15 portraits are dealt out through the landscapes one at a time after runs of two, three or four landscapes in a fixed irregular rhythm, so neither orientation bunches up; each orientation keeps its own gallery order.
- **Layout**: one stack down the centre line of the page. Every work is centred; widths cycle through a set of fractions of a 560px column (100%, 68%, 86%, 60%, 78%, 92%, 64%; portraits a further 22% narrower), so the sizes keep changing; each work is pulled up over the one before it by 4.5% of the column (about 25px) so they float over each other; stacking order follows size, so a smaller work sits on top of the larger ones above and below it, with a soft deep shadow. On phones the column is the content width and the overlap 10%.
- **Parallax**: as the stack scrolls, each work drifts by its own depth (the smaller, front pieces faster than the larger ones behind: a 10% to 32% share of the scroll, measured from the middle of the screen), so the layers separate and the pieces feel afloat.
- **Reveal**: each work is a window onto a picture 14% larger than it, which slides inside the window as the work crosses the screen (fully up when the work enters at the bottom, fully down by the time it reaches the top), so a little more of the painting's top or bottom comes into view as you scroll, as on cathydolle.com. The cost is that about 7% of each edge of the painting is outside the window at any moment.
- **Scrolling**: the gallery is its own scroll area under a fixed header. Mouse wheel and trackpad glide (eased); click-and-drag anywhere moves the column and coasts on release (a drag never opens a work); touch screens scroll natively; keyboard scrolling works. The footer ends the column.
- **Scrollbar**: no native bar. A hairline track on the bag icon's line (the page's right side padding), running from 24px below the header to 24px above the bottom of the screen, with the Tree of Life emblem (the favicon drawing, 58px tall, at 80%, full strength on hover) as the handle, centred on the track; the handle is a fixed size rather than a proportional bar. It fades in while the pointer is over the gallery and fades out when it leaves. Drag the emblem or click the track to move. Hidden on touch screens.
- **Hover**: the work grows to 103% (0.45s, soft ease) and comes to the front of the stack with a deeper shadow, and its title fades in beside it, 56px off the work's edge and vertically centred: to the right of odd works, to the left of even ones, alternating down the stack (EB Garamond italic capitals, with a small "View details" cue beneath; mouse only, not shown on phones). **Click** opens the detail view (`Gallery.dc.html#<file>`). `#<file>` or `#<title>` in the URL opens the page scrolled to that work.

## Gallery, filmstrip version (`gallery-filmstrip.html`)

- Standalone plain HTML/CSS/JS page (not templated) with the same header, footer, side padding and mobile menu. Header, work and filmstrip fill the screen exactly, with the thumbnails 20px above the bottom edge; the footer sits just below the fold and is reached by scrolling (the mobile menu therefore runs to the bottom of the screen), except on phones in portrait, where the footer stays on screen and the header, work and filmstrip share the height above it.
- **Stage**: the selected work shown whole, as large as the window allows: it fills the height between header and filmstrip (16px below the header) and, from 768px up, may run wider than the page's usual side padding, out to 3% of the screen width each side with the chevrons still hugging it, centred on the off-white page with the usual soft shadow; works change with a soft 0.9s crossfade (arrows, thumbnails, keys and swipes alike): the incoming image is fully loaded first, then fades in over the outgoing one (no insignia behind it); the works either side of the current one are preloaded so the arrows respond at once. First work (Brilliance) on load, or the work named in the URL hash.
- **Previous / next chevrons** sit either side of the work (the same thin light-grey chevrons as the home carousel, darker on hover, drawn as a hairline; 39 x 108px, 20 x 56px on phones), hugging the work with a fixed gap and vertically centred on it. Click or tap steps to the previous / next work in the line-up, wrapping round at either end, and glides that work's thumbnail to the centre of the strip. The work leaves room for them (84px each side, 32px on phones).
- **Hover** on the work enlarges it gently (103%) and shows its title (black EB Garamond italic capitals, matching the detail page) vertically centred in the white space between the work and the thumbnails at every window shape and a small "View details" cue (always visible on touch screens). **Click** opens that work's detail view in the main gallery (`Gallery.dc.html#<file>`; the gallery's deep link accepts a title or a file name, so the several "Untitled" works resolve correctly).
- **Filmstrip**: every visible work in one row anchored above the footer, thumbnails 38px tall (31px on phones) with 6px gaps (files in `assets/thumbs/`, 260px tall so they stay sharp when magnified), edges faded. The row is a track moved by an eased transform and steered by the cursor only while it is on the thumbnails themselves (the thumbnail row, or a magnified thumbnail rising above it; hovering the artwork, the title or the space around the row does nothing): in the middle 10% of the page width the row eases to a complete stop; left or right of that zone it drifts that way, faster the further out the cursor is, up to 380px per second (about three and a half thumbnails a second) near the page edges; it stops at either end and coasts to rest when the cursor leaves the strip. Speed is the same on 60Hz and 120Hz screens (`CRUISE`, `DEAD`, `ACCEL` in the page script). The wheel/trackpad also moves the row, directly and without the speed limit; choosing a work with the arrow keys or a swipe glides its thumbnail to the centre. Thumbnails magnify Dock-style under the cursor (up to about 190%, bell-curve falloff, growing up from the baseline, neighbours sliding aside, the one under the cursor staying under it) and relax when the cursor leaves the strip. Touch screens get the same glide and magnification, driven by the finger: the row moves and thumbnails swell only while a finger is on the filmstrip, it coasts to rest when the finger lifts, and a touch anywhere else on the page stops it dead. Mouse events synthesised from taps are ignored, so phones that wrongly report a mouse behave correctly; the title is always shown once a touch is seen. Click a thumbnail to show that work; the active one is outlined (the outline hugs the image, no gap), the rest dimmed. Arrow keys and finger swipes on the stage step through works.
- The works list is embedded in the page (generated from `Gallery.dc.html`, hidden works left out) and needs regenerating when the gallery's list changes.

## Detail view ("Original")

- Two columns on desktop and tablet: image left, text right; stacked on phones. Text is vertically centred on the image; whole block centred in the space below the back link.
- **Image size**: the work (frame included) is never taller than 84% of the screen height less the header, so vertical works stop short of the bottom of the screen with clear space above and below instead of running off it; it is centred in its column. Landscape works still fill the column width unless that would make them taller than this.
- **Text position** (desktop and tablet): the text block sits at the midpoint of the white space between the artwork's right edge and the content's right edge, so it reads close to the work (65px from a landscape, about 105px from a vertical on a 1440px screen) rather than out at the bag icon's line.
- Text: "Original", italic title (all work titles on the site are italic), medium, size, then two icon lines (limited edition set; certificate of authenticity). On tablet-portrait widths (600–767px) the two icon lines sit in a right-hand column. "Email to enquire" and "Buy the print" links at 11px.
- **Framing carousel**: three dots below the image (Unframed / White frame / Black frame). Frames are drawn in code (16px border + white mount) and ease on/off over 0.5s. Tap the right half of the image or swipe left for next, left half or swipe right for previous.
- **Magnified view** (zoom button below the dots, desktop/tablet): a white lightbox covering the page showing only the artwork with the framing dots. Double-click / double-tap steps the zoom 1x → 2x → 3x → 1x, keeping the clicked point in view (the lightbox scrolls); option-click steps it back down. Arrow keys flip frames, swipe works, Escape or × closes.
