## Global layout

- **Header and footer are identical on every page**: same height, same side distance (the standard side padding below), including the home page, where the painting may be wider or narrower than the header.
- **Side padding** (header, content, footer share it): 24px phone, 48px tablet (768–1023px), 10% of the viewport from 1024px up. A stable scrollbar gutter keeps pages aligned whether or not they scroll.
- **Header**: 72px tall, growing to 80px between 1280px and 1920px wide. Logo, nav links (13px, tracked uppercase) and account/bag icons share one baseline on desktop. Active nav item is bold, no underline. Phone header is vertically centred. Account and bag icons are thin line icons (a person outline and a square shopping bag) from `assets/icons/account.svg` and `cart.svg`, drawn at 19px in the header (80% of their 24px design size, line weight kept visually the same) and at 24px on the mobile menu rows; `assets/icons/mail.svg` (solid grey envelope) is stored for later use and not placed anywhere yet.
- **Footer**: 43px tall, growing to 54px between 1280px and 1920px (15% shorter than the earlier 50–64px); on phones held in portrait it is twice as tall (86px), content still centred. One line: copyright left, Instagram (links to instagram.com/arbor.eye, new tab) and Pinterest icons right; on very narrow phones the copyright wraps to two lines with "Arbor & Eye" kept together on the second, and stays vertically centred on the icons. Sits at the end of the page (not sticky).
- **Mobile menu** (under 768px): hamburger becomes an X; the menu slides down as a fixed overlay between header and footer, footer pinned to the bottom of the screen, page scroll locked. Items: Gallery, About, Contact, Shipping, Returns & Exchanges, then Login/Account and Shopping Cart rows with icons (account/bag icons leave the header whenever the hamburger shows). Double spacing between items.
- **Fonts**: Neuzeit Grotesk (self-hosted in `assets/fonts/`, loaded through `assets/fonts/neuzeit.css`; Light 300, Regular 400, Bold 700, with 600 resolving to Bold) for all sans text, falling back to Helvetica Neue; EB Garamond (Google Fonts) for headings and work titles.
- **Logo** (back to the original since 2026-10-12, Ned's preference; the Tree of Life in the logo is to be revisited): the ARBOR & EYE wordmark with the original engraved Tree of Life standing through it, its own ampersand taking the place of the letter ampersand: `uploads/logo-tol.svg`, a still on every page, the homepage included. ## Gallery, multi-up filmstrip (`gallery-filmstrip-2.html`, "G5")

- A copy of the filmstrip gallery (thumbnails smaller here: 30px tall, 25px on phones, magnifying to about 150% under the cursor rather than 190%; the row drifts slowly so a thumbnail is easy to catch: it stays still while the cursor is in the middle 30% of the page, then speeds up gently towards the edges (squared, so about 13px per second three-quarters of the way out) to at most 120px per second at the very edge, rather than 380; it steers only while the cursor moves: once the cursor rests on a thumbnail the row brakes to a stop within about a second so it can be clicked; the chevrons scale with the viewport from 39 x 108px at 1440px down to 20 x 56px, and on phones sit on the page's side padding, level with the logo and the menu button) with a **view switch** at the top left, lined up with the logo (where the grid gallery's switcher sits) (three icons: one solid square, three vertical bars, and a 4 x 3 grid of small squares; the active one black, the rest pale; hidden on phones, which stay one-up). It sets how many works are featured: 1 (exactly the filmstrip page's behaviour), 3 in a row (the default on load), or 12 in three rows of four.
- The featured works form a centred row (or a three-row grid), all the same height, 28px apart. The chevrons sit in the same place in every view (one, three and twelve): where the three-up row puts them, just outside a row 85% of the content width (142-226px and 1214-1298px on a 1440px screen); one-up never grows past them. One-up is 86% of the largest the stage allows and sits 14px above centre, for more room around the hover title; 3-up's row spans 85% of the content width (centred), with the three works drawn at 95% of what that would allow and the freed width going into the two gaps between them (about 51px apart on a 1440px screen), and 12-up is about 81% of the space with its four columns 60px apart (each work fitted inside its 3:2 box, never trimmed) for more room around the works, so the first work's left edge lines up with the logo and the last work's right edge with the bag icon (unless the works are so tall that the height limits them first). In the grid the rows are 64px apart so a hovered work's title fits beneath it without touching the row below; every landscape is drawn in the same 1.492:1 box so the columns line up (the few wider works, such as Brilliance and Canopy, are trimmed a little at the sides there only), and portraits keep their own narrower shape, centred in the column. The four columns are always the landscape box wide whatever mix of orientations a page holds, so portraits line up on the same four column centres as landscapes and the grid, and its arrows, never shift from page to page. When the window moves (arrows, arrow keys, swipes or a thumbnail click) the row slides: the works travel in the direction of the arrow (right arrow: the current works slide off to the right and the next set arrives from the left; left arrow the reverse; since 2026-10-07 the arrows and arrow keys slide vertically instead: the right arrow sends the current set up and out while the next set rises in from the bottom, the left arrow drops the previous set in from the top; since 2026-10-09 thumbnail taps move vertically too (a later work: the row goes up and the new set rises from below; an earlier one: down, the new set dropping in from above); only dragging moves sideways. Before that, a thumbnail click went sideways: a work to the right of the featured set slides the row left, arriving from the right, and a work to the left slides it right), together like a belt (1.3s on a soft ease that starts gently, glides and settles slowly; the incoming images are loaded before anything moves); changing the view crossfades the slots in place instead. Hovering any featured work enlarges it gently (103%) and shows its title directly beneath that work, 25px below it when several works are shown (midway to the thumbnails on one-up; the title slides across as the cursor moves between works), and clicking (in any view: one, three or twelve) opens its detail view, `Gallery.dc.html#<file>`; a drag never opens a work.
- The featured set is a window over the list: every filmstrip thumbnail is at full strength (no dimming, no outline); the thumbnails of the works currently featured carry a deeper drop shadow (0 4px 12px at 38%, easing in) than the rest (0 2px 8px at 12%). Clicking a thumbnail slides the window to it (that work becomes the leftmost, unless it is within the last few, when the window stops at the end of the list). Changing the count keeps the first featured work and extends or trims the window from there. Arrows, arrow keys, and dragging anywhere on the stage (mouse or finger, including the white space around the works: the row follows the pointer, a pull of more than 40px slides on in that direction, a shorter pull springs back) move the window by a whole set (three works at 3-up, twelve at 12-up); one-up wraps round at the ends, wider windows stop at them. Scrolling up or down over the works (mouse wheel or trackpad) moves the window the same way: down is the right arrow (the next set rises from below), up the left arrow; one set per gesture (further scrolling is ignored until the wheel rests for 200ms, momentum included, and the slide has finished). The URL hash names the first featured work.
- **Touch screens**: the hover title (always shown on touch) and the chevrons hide while anything moves (a slide or crossfade, a swipe, the filmstrip, page scrolling) and reappear once a work is at rest.

## Gallery, editorial column (`gallery-editorial.html`, "G6")

- Standalone plain HTML/CSS/JS page with the same header, footer, side padding and mobile menu; the works list is embedded (same list as the filmstrip) and needs regenerating when the gallery's list changes.
- **Opening** (desktop and tablet; skipped on phones, for reduced motion, and when the URL names a work): the first twelve works appear one by one as small dark tiles spread evenly across 80% of the screen in a horizontal row, with clear space between them (0 - 1s); the row turns 90 degrees clockwise into an evenly spaced column (1 - 2s); each tile then grows toward the viewer into its own place in the stack, only now overlapping (2 - 3.2s); the tiles turn into the paintings (2.9 - 3.5s) and hand over to the real column. About 4 seconds in all; a click anywhere skips to the end. Modelled on the opening of cathydolle.com. Plain JavaScript (one timeline driving the transforms frame by frame), so it ports to Shopify as is.
- **Order**: Brilliance opens and Canopy closes (as in the grid); between them the 15 portraits are dealt out through the landscapes one at a time after runs of two, three or four landscapes in a fixed irregular rhythm, so neither orientation bunches up; each orientation keeps its own gallery order.
- **Layout**: one stack down the centre line of the page. Every work is centred; sizes are fairly even, varying gently: landscapes 82-100% of a 560px column (about 460-560px wide), portraits drawn at the same area as a landscape of that step (about 310-375px wide), following a repeating rhythm (100%, 68%, 86%, 60%, 78%, 92%, 64%) that also sets each work's parallax depth and stacking; at rest each work rides up over the one before it by 12% of its own height, so they float over each other; stacking order follows that rhythm, so a work further down it sits on top of the larger ones above and below it, with a soft deep shadow. Every painting shows whole (frames share the scans' own proportions; nothing is cropped).
- **Parallax** (motion-based): while the column scrolls, each work trails the scroll by its own amount, slowly and softly (the smaller, nearer pieces keep up more closely, the larger ones behind lag more; drift eases into a 90px limit rather than stopping hard), and the wheel glides on a long, soft ease, so the layers drift apart and the pieces feel afloat; when scrolling stops they all settle back into the stack with the 12% overlap.
- **Scrolling**: the gallery is its own scroll area under a fixed header. Mouse wheel and trackpad glide (eased); click-and-drag anywhere moves the column and coasts on release (a drag never opens a work); touch screens scroll natively; keyboard scrolling works. The footer ends the column.
- **Scrollbar**: no native bar. A 2px track on the bag icon's line (the page's right side padding), running from 24px below the header to 24px above the bottom of the screen, with the simplified Tree of Life (`uploads/tol-v3.svg`, the same v3 mark as the logo; 58px tall, at 80%, full strength on hover) as the handle, 4px right of the track's centre; the handle is a fixed size rather than a proportional bar. It fades in whenever the pointer is anywhere on the page (header included) and fades out when the pointer leaves the window. Drag the emblem or click the track to move. Hidden on touch screens.
- **Hover**: the work grows to 120% (0.45s, soft ease) and comes to the front of every work (above all the others' stacking levels; phones' tapped work likewise) with a deeper shadow, while every work above it draws up and every work below it draws down just far enough to leave 60px clear above and below the enlarged work (they ease back when the pointer leaves). The last work hovered stays in front of its neighbours after the pointer leaves (back at its normal size), until another work is hovered. Its title fades in beside it, 56px off the work's edge and vertically centred: to the right of odd works, to the left of even ones, alternating down the stack (EB Garamond italic capitals, the title alone; mouse only, not shown on phones). **Click** opens the detail view (`Gallery.dc.html#<file>`). `#<file>` or `#<title>` in the URL opens the page scrolled to that work.
- **Phones**: widths range only from 80% to 100% of the column (portraits at two thirds of that width, for the same area), so no work is more than 25% larger or smaller than another; the overlap is 70% of desktop's (8.4% of each work's height).
